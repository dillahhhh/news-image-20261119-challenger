## Judge criteria (from competition.yaml — the actual instructions)
1. One day's news, names that day (specific events, date named).
2. Selection and breadth: most consequential stories, across regions and fields.
3. It reads: viewer can tell what happened from the image alone, each story clear from its picture + short labels; nothing self-contradictory, implausible, or known false.
4. Faithful: as reported, no hype, no speculation as fact.
5. Craft: one clear composition, cleanly drawn/rendered, legible at ~300px, no smears/artefacts/garbled text.
"Detail for its own sake does not count." Scoring: pairwise, margin 1-5; Bradley-Terry fit; playoff top 52.
