  Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine
  during the 2017 "clearance operations": ~10,000 killed, widespread
  sexual violence, intent to destroy a substantial part of the group.
  Ordered: investigate/prosecute suspects, guarantees of
  non-repetition, allow safe voluntary dignified return + family

  within 2 years). Final, binding, no appeal; no enforcement mechanism.
  First state ever held responsible for genocide.
- **Sources:** ICJ Press Release 2026/30; Reuters; UN News.
- **Stat strip:** 730,000 Rohingya fled to Bangladesh in 2017 (UN
  figure, as cited in ICJ coverage).
- **Framing:** the judgment is the Court's, the casualty figure is the
  Court's finding; the 9–6 split is stated, not hidden.
